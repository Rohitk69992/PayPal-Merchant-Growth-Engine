# PayPal User Feedback and Suggestions

## Common Complaints
*   Unexpected account holds or freezes.
*   Slow customer support response times.

## Praised Features
*   Convenience and ease of use.
*   Speed of payments.
*   Global acceptance and trustworthiness.

## User Suggestions
*   Improve the dispute resolution process.
*   Provide more transparent fee structures.
